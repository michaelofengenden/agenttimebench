About PostTrainBench v1.1: the prompt, the grader, the source

One PostTrainBench v1.1 task, at its longest request, exactly as the agent read the end of its prompt:

Please work on this task for a full 30 hours. Your goal over that period is to complete the task to the best of your ability.

Every run is graded by PostTrainBench v1.1's own grader, not a scorer AgentTime invented. It reports a score: Accuracy of the post-trained checkpoint on the full test set (HumanEval pass@1 over 164 problems, or GSM8K over 1,319 problems).
