About PaperBench: the prompt, the grader, the source

Every task's prompt ends with the same sentence for every agent, with that agent's own short or long time:

Please work on this task for a full [time]. Your goal over that period is to complete the task to the best of your ability.

Every run is graded by PaperBench's own grader, not a scorer AgentTime invented. It reports a score: Replication score: a weighted rubric of pass/fail leaf checks, 0 to 1, after a full reproduction attempt.
