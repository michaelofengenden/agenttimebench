Read before quoting: 5 notes
  1. On ProgramBench, Claude Fable 5.1 refused 6 runs within 30 seconds instead of starting the task. Counting those as very fast finishes would make Fable look like it chose to stop early, so they are left out of the chart and the numbers by default. That is why Fable has 36 ProgramBench runs where the others have 42. How refusals are handled
  2. 4 runs are archived: no clock tool was recorded. They are drawn hollow and counted in every number. Details
  3. 61 runs were stopped by AgentTime, so their time is a lower bound. Details
  4. 76 timed runs have an ending not labelled yet. Details
  5. The paper also ran Claude Fable 5.1 inside Codex on a 34-task subset, to see whether the harness explains its timing. It did not: Claude Fable 5.1 still did not track the requested time in either harness. The harness-swap result
