AgentTime

Measuring Time Awareness in AI agents

200 tasks from 18 benchmarksv1.1, 600 runs per agent and three tests: duration following, forecasting and retrospection.

Duration following

Does an AI agent work for as long as it is asked? Each of the 200 tasks was asked for a short and a long duration.

Two speeds

MiMo V2.6 Pro UltraSpeed is MiMo V2.6 Pro served faster. With no time asked it finished the same tasks in under half the time, yet forecast them about the same, so it stopped sooner at every ask.
