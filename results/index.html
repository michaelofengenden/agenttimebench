Reading the marks

Across is the time asked for and up is the time worked, both on log scales. A mark on the target line worked exactly as long as asked, and the pale band around it is within 5% of the time asked.

Capped marks were stopped by AgentTime, so their time is a lower bound. Runs asked for the same time are spread slightly sideways so they do not hide each other.
