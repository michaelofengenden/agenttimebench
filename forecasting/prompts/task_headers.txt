# staged files (except ProgramBench, YC-Bench, Agents Last Exam and OSWorld 2.0), and runs of three or more newlines
# became two; the seventh pattern was applied to ALE-Bench only. The Frontier-Bench files keep their closing
# "You have N seconds to complete this task." sentence (the no-request Harbor runs removed it from instruction.md:
# run_harbor_sol.sh), and the PaperBench files keep "allotted time" and "maximum runtime".
